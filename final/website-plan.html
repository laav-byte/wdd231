Website Project Site Plan

Site Name

REE Examineers' Resources

This name was chosen because it tells who the audience and what it could provide to them

Site Purpose

The purpose is to provide inspirational messages and to give list of available review centers.

Scenarios

  • How can I hold on to achieve my goal despite of the struggles that I have?
  • What are the available review centers that I could choose to enroll to?

Color Scheme

Primary Color (#546B41): Used for headings, buttons, and highlights.

Secondary Color (#FFF8EC): Used for background and section areas.

Typography

Poppins: Used for headings.

Roboto: Used for body text.

Wireframe

Mobile View

Header
Hero Section
Property Cards (stacked)
Footer

Desktop View

Header (Logo + Navigation)
Hero Section
Grid of Property Cards (3 columns)
Footer
