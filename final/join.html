Professional Electrical Engineer

Professional Electrical Engineers are authorized to design, sign, and seal complex electrical plans, perform advanced system analysis, and take full responsibility for massive electrical installations.

Registered Electrical Engineer

A Registered Electrical Engineer (REE) in the Philippines is a licensed professional authorized to handle, supervise, and design electrical systems. Governed by RA 7920, their scope includes power plants, manufacturing facilities, and commercial buildings.

REE Reviewee

REE reviewees are aspiring Registered Electrical Engineers (REE) who are enrolled in specialized review programs to prepare for the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) board examination
